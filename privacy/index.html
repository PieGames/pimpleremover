Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Piegames ("we") makes and operates the Android app Pimple Remover (the "App", package name com.fgsoft.pimpleeraser). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Your photos stay on your device

You choose a photo with the Android photo picker, so the App can see only the photo you pick. All editing (finding pimples, skin smoothing, teeth whitening, dark circles, brighter eyes) runs on your device with models built into the App. Your photos and face data are not uploaded to us or to any third party. When you tap Save, the edited photo is saved to your device's gallery (Pictures/Pimple Remover).

2. No accounts

The App has no sign-up or login. We do not receive information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or address. Your settings and whether you bought Remove ads are stored only on your device.

4. Your choices

  • Ad and analytics consent: In regions that require it, a consent form appears. You can change your choice later in Settings › Ad consent settings (shown only where required).
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Data on your device: Uninstalling the App removes its settings stored on your device. Photos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them. A Remove ads purchase can be restored from Settings after reinstalling.

5. Retention

  • Analytics events are kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics, at most 14 months.
  • Information received by AdMob, AppLovin and Google Play is kept according to their own policies.
  • Support emails are kept for one year after we reply, then deleted.

6. Third parties and international transfers

The processors above (Google LLC, AppLovin Corporation) may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their policies: Google · Google advertising · Firebase · AppLovin. We do not sell your information.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of information we hold. Because we hold almost no information that identifies you directly, we may need your device's app instance ID to find it. Contact us at the address below.
